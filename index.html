About

I am a Chemoinformatics M.Sc. student at the University of Strasbourg, working where physical chemistry, molecular modelling and machine learning meet.

My work covers the full QSPR workflow: curating experimental data from chemical databases, computing and selecting molecular descriptors, training and validating ensemble models, and turning them into documented tools that non-specialists can use. I build and maintain open-source Python software for ionic liquid data and properties, including pyionics and ILQSPR.

I am particularly interested in data-driven design of ionic liquids and sustainable solvents.

Research interests

  • QSPR modelling and machine learning for physicochemical properties
  • Ionic liquids and sustainable solvents
  • Curation of experimental chemical data
  • Open-source scientific software
  • Computational chemistry (DFT)

  2. Chemical space analysis of ionic liquids

    M1 research internship · With Dr. Shamkhal Baybekov · 2026

    Curated NIST ILThermo into clean density, refractive index and melting point datasets and mapped their chemical space with Mordred descriptors, scaffolds and UMAP.

  3. ILQSPR: QSPR modelling of ionic liquid-solvent mixtures

    With Dr. Shamkhal Baybekov · 2025 - present · Manuscript in preparation

    Consensus XGBoost models that predict the density and refractive index of ionic liquids, pure and mixed with water, ethanol or isopropanol (R2 = 0.90-0.96).
