Presentation Visual Audit
Paste your slide text (one slide per --- block) and get a per-slide audit against good-practice rules: word count, bullet count, the 6×6 rule, reading level, and ALL-CAPS or wall-of-text warnings. Find the slides that need trimming before you rehearse. Local.
Guidelines used: ≤ ~6 bullets and ≤ ~6 words per bullet (6×6 rule), ≤ ~40 words total per slide, avoid ALL-CAPS lines, and keep the reading grade conversational. These are heuristics — break them deliberately when it serves the talk.
