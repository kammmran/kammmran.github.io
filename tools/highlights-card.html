Research Highlights / Social Card Creator
Announce a new paper with a sharp social-media card. Drop in the title, a one-line key finding, authors, journal and a couple of bullet highlights, then export a ready-to-post PNG sized for Twitter/X, LinkedIn, or Instagram. Great for the lab's "new preprint!" post. Local, no upload.
Tip: keep the title under ~12 words so it stays readable in a feed.
