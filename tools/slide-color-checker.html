Slide Color Accessibility
Presentation Color Accessibility Checker
Check the colours in your slide palette for accessibility: a contrast matrix of every text-on-background pair (with WCAG AA/AAA for normal and large text), plus a colour-blind preview of the swatches. Make sure your slides are legible from the back row and for the ~8% with colour-vision deficiency. Local.
Contrast matrix (row text on column background)
Colour-blind preview
WCAG thresholds: AA needs 4.5:1 (normal) or 3:1 (large/bold ≥24px); AAA needs 7:1 / 4.5:1. Slides are viewed at distance, so prefer pairs comfortably above AA. The colour-blind rows simulate protan/deutan/tritan vision — if two palette colours collapse together, don't rely on them alone to distinguish data.
