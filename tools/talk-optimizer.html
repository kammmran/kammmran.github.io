Conference Talk Slide Optimizer
Make your deck fit the clock. Enter your talk length and paste the slides; the tool estimates speaking time per slide from its content, flags slides that will run long or short, and tells you the target slide count and whether to cut or add. Built for the strict 12- or 15-minute conference slot. Local.
A common rule of thumb is ~1 slide per minute, but content-dense slides take longer. The estimate adds a fixed transition cost per slide plus speaking time for the words. Rehearse with a timer to calibrate your real pace.
