Author Contribution Generator (CRediT)
Build the "Author Contributions" statement most journals now require, using the 14 standardised CRediT roles. Tick which authors did what in the matrix, then copy a clean statement (and an optional degree-of-contribution note). Local - nothing is uploaded.
CRediT (Contributor Roles Taxonomy) is the standard adopted by Elsevier, PLOS, Cell Press and many others. Tick a cell where that author meaningfully contributed to that role.
