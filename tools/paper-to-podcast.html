Paper-to-Podcast Converter
Paper-to-Podcast Converter
Turn a paper into a two-host podcast-style conversation and listen to it narrated by two distinct voices. Paste your text, generate the dialogue script, then press play - a friendly way to review a paper on a commute.
The script is built locally with simple heuristics - it paraphrases lightly and adds conversational connective tissue, but it doesn't fact-check or add information beyond your text. Read through before relying on it.
