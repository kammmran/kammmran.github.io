Protein Sequence Analyzer
Paste an amino-acid sequence and compute its molecular weight, theoretical isoelectric point (pI), net charge at a chosen pH, GRAVY hydropathy, aromaticity, and full residue composition. Accepts FASTA. A quick ProtParam-style readout that runs entirely in your browser.
MW uses average isotopic masses; pI/charge use standard pKa values (EMBOSS set) and are theoretical estimates for the unmodified sequence. GRAVY > 0 suggests hydrophobic, < 0 hydrophilic.
