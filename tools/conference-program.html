Conference Program Designer
Type your schedule in a simple text format and get a clean, printable conference program. Days, sessions, time slots, speakers and rooms are parsed and laid out automatically, with session headers and break styling. Print to PDF or export. Everything stays in your browser.
Syntax: # Title · ## Day heading · = Session title | Room · time | item | speaker | room. Add | break at the end of a line to style it as a break/meal.
