Figure Consistency Checker
Drop all of a manuscript's figures at once and check that they're consistent with each other: matching widths, similar effective DPI, the same file format and colour mode, and comparable background tone. Mismatched figures are the tell-tale sign of a rushed submission — this catches them before a reviewer does. Local.
Consistency, not perfection: figures legitimately differ in height and content. The checker flags the things that look careless side by side — a JPEG among PNGs, one low-DPI panel, a grayscale figure in a colour set, or a dark-background plot among white ones.
