Dimensional Analysis Checker
Check whether an equation is dimensionally consistent. Define your variables with SI dimensions, type a formula, and the tool resolves the dimensions of each side in terms of M, L, T, I, Θ, N, J — flagging mismatches. Catches unit errors before they cost you. Local.
Known quantities: velocity, acceleration, force, energy, power, pressure, momentum, charge, frequency, area, volume, density, voltage, current… Base SI symbols: M (mass), L (length), T (time), I (current), Θ (temperature), N (amount), J (luminous). Dimensionless = 1.
