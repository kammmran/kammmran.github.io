Data-viz Palette
Colorblind-safe palettes for charts, maps, and figures. Sequential, diverging, categorical.
Tips for accessible data viz
  • Categorical - pick distinct hues. Limit to ~6–8 categories per chart.
  • Sequential - one hue, varying lightness. Good for ordered data (e.g. heatmaps).
  • Diverging - two hues meeting at a neutral middle. Use when zero (or a midpoint) is meaningful.
  • Cyclic - wraps from start to end smoothly. Good for phase, time-of-day, or angles.
  • Avoid jet/rainbow palettes - they're not perceptually uniform and confuse color-blind viewers.
  • Pair color with shape, label, or pattern so charts read in black & white too.
