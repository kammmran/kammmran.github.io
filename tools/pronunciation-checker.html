Pronunciation Checker
Scientific Pronunciation Checker
Practise tricky technical terms. Hear the reference pronunciation, say it back, and get a match score based on what speech recognition heard. Great for unfamiliar drug names, taxa, and theory jargon before a talk.
Scoring compares the recognizer's transcription to your target word-by-word and by character similarity. Recognition leans toward common words, so rare jargon may score low even when you nailed it — use the score as a rough guide, not a verdict.
