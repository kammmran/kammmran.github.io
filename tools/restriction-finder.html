Restriction Enzyme Finder
Find restriction sites in a DNA sequence. Scans a panel of common enzymes (including IUPAC-ambiguous recognition sites), reports the cut positions and fragment sizes, and highlights enzymes that cut once - handy for cloning. Linear or circular. Local.
Panel of ~30 common enzymes with degenerate-base matching. Cut position is at the enzyme's nick on the top strand. For a complete enzyme catalogue use REBASE; this covers the workhorses.
