Booklet Layout Optimizer
Plan a print booklet before you typeset it. Paste your text, choose page size, font and columns, and get an estimated page count, a signature check (pages should be a multiple of 4 for saddle-stitch), and suggestions to hit a target length. Pure estimation maths - nothing leaves your browser.
Estimates assume continuous body text in a single typeface; real layouts vary with headings, figures and white space, so treat the page count as ±15%. The signature check matters for saddle-stitch binding - printers need the total page count to be a multiple of 4.
