Computational Cost Estimator
Estimate the compute, wall-clock time, energy and carbon footprint of a training run. Enter model size, dataset size, epochs and your GPU; the tool uses the standard 6·N·D FLOPs rule and a hardware-utilisation factor to project cost - useful for grant budgets and the "compute & carbon" statement reviewers now ask for. Local.
Uses FLOPs ≈ 6 · N · D (forward+backward) for training, divided by the GPU's peak throughput × utilisation. Real utilisation is often 30–50%. Carbon = energy × grid intensity; look up your region's gCO₂/kWh for accuracy.
