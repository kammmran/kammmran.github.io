PDF Proceedings Merger
Multi-PDF Proceedings Merger
Combine many PDFs - individual papers, a cover, front matter - into one proceedings file. Drag to reorder, and optionally insert a generated table-of-contents page with computed page ranges and PDF bookmarks. Merging happens entirely in your browser; your files are never uploaded.
    Drag the handle to reorder before merging. Uses the open-source pdf-lib library loaded from a CDN; the PDFs themselves stay on your machine. Encrypted/password-protected PDFs must be unlocked first.
