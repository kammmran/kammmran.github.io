Primer Design Assistant
Primer Design Assistant
Design PCR primers from a template. The tool scans candidate forward and reverse primers within your length and Tm window, scores them on GC content, GC clamp, self-complementarity and runs, and reports the resulting amplicon. Tm is computed with the nearest-neighbour model. Local.
A screening aid, not a replacement for Primer3 - it doesn't check the primers against a whole genome for specificity. Always BLAST candidate primers before ordering.
