Q&A Preparation Assistant
Anticipate the questions you'll get after a talk. Paste your abstract or summary; the tool generates a checklist of likely audience questions - on limitations, methods, generalisability, comparisons, and future work - adapted to cues in your text, with space to jot your answers and print a prep sheet. Heuristic, local.
These are generic-but-targeted prompts derived from common reviewer/audience patterns plus keywords in your text - not model-generated. Use them to rehearse; the best prep is knowing your own limitations cold.
