About
Roughly 8% of men and 0.5% of women have some form of color vision deficiency. The most common is red–green (deuteranomaly/protanomaly). Use this tool to check whether your design - chart, slide, or interface - still communicates without color, and consider pairing color with shape, label, or pattern.
