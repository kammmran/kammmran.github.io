Multi-Panel Figure Creator
Assemble several images into a single publication-ready composite figure with automatic A, B, C panel labels. Choose the grid, spacing, background and label style, then export a high-resolution PNG. Reorder panels by dragging. Runs entirely in your browser.
Drag thumbnails to reorder panels. Each cell is sized to the largest panel's aspect for a clean grid; use "Cover" to fill cells edge-to-edge (crops), or "Contain" to show whole images (letterboxed).
