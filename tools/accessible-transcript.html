Accessible Transcript Converter
Audio-to-Accessible Transcript Converter
Clean a raw transcript into an accessibility-compliant one: consistent speaker labels, sentence casing and punctuation tidy-up, normalized non-speech cues like [inaudible] and [laughter], and export to plain text, Markdown, or WebVTT captions.
Follows common accessibility conventions (speaker IDs, sound descriptions in brackets, one idea per line). Always do a final human review — accurate transcripts are a legal accessibility requirement in many contexts.
