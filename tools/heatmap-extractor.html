Heatmap Value Extractor
Read numeric values back out of a colour heatmap. Calibrate the colour scale by clicking its low and high ends and entering their values, then hover or click anywhere on the heatmap to recover the underlying number from its colour. Sample a whole grid to CSV. Local, no upload.
Low High Click the lightest/lowest end of the colour bar, then the highest end.

Works best on continuous colour maps (viridis, jet, hot, blue-white-red). Recovery maps each pixel's colour to the nearest point along the calibrated low→high colour path, so it's approximate near anti-aliased edges and text. For a grid sample, set rows/cols matching the heatmap cells and it reads each cell centre.
