Image Metadata Inspector
Image Metadata Inspector
Read the hidden EXIF/metadata embedded in an image: camera and software used, capture date, editing-software tags, stored DPI, GPS, and more. Useful for integrity checks and for spotting when a "raw" figure was actually processed in Photoshop. Nothing is uploaded - parsing happens in your browser.
EXIF is richest in JPEG and TIFF straight from cameras. Many exported figures and screenshots carry little or no EXIF - an absence of metadata is normal and not itself suspicious. Note: software stripping metadata is trivial, so presence is more informative than absence.
