Subject / Keyword Index Generator
Build a back-of-book subject index. Paste your entries (each tagged with a page or entry number) and either supply your own keyword list or let the tool suggest salient terms; it then lists every location each term appears. Alphabetical, deduplicated, printable. Local processing.
Matching is case-insensitive and whole-word, with simple plural folding. For multi-word terms, type them exactly. Auto-suggest ranks repeated noun-like phrases - review and prune before finalizing.
