Duplicate & Similar Figure Detector
Drop a batch of figures and find duplicates or near-duplicates — even when resized, lightly cropped, or re-saved. Uses perceptual hashing (dHash + aHash) so visually identical images cluster together regardless of file format. Handy for screening a manuscript's figures or a reviewer checking for reused panels. All local.
Lower Hamming distance = more similar (0 = identical). Around ≤10 of 128 bits catches resizes and re-saves; raise it to find looser matches. Perceptual hashing flags visual similarity — confirm flagged pairs by eye, since legitimately similar figures (e.g. control vs. treatment) can also match.
