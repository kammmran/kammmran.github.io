Methodology Flowchart Generator
Turn a numbered list of steps into a clean methodology or experiment-workflow flowchart. Write one step per line, use indentation for sub-steps and ? for decisions; the tool lays out boxes and arrows automatically. Export as SVG (vector, editable) or PNG. Also builds PRISMA-style screening diagrams.
Syntax: a normal line is a process box. A line ending in ? becomes a diamond decision; the next lines starting with -> (Yes) and => (No) become its branches that rejoin the main flow. SVG export stays crisp at any size and can be edited in Illustrator/Inkscape.
