Image Forensics (ELA) Analyzer
Screen a figure for signs of manipulation using Error Level Analysis. The tool re-compresses the image and amplifies the difference: spliced, cloned, or pasted regions often compress differently and light up against a uniform background. Pair it with the noise and luminance-gradient views to spot inconsistencies. A first-pass screening aid - not proof.
Reading ELA: an authentic JPEG tends toward a uniform error level; sharp edges naturally show higher error. A pasted region from a higher-quality source, or a re-saved patch, can appear brighter or with a different texture than its surroundings. Resaving or screenshotting an image destroys these traces, so a clean ELA does not prove authenticity. Always corroborate before making claims.
