Student Participation Analyzer
Measure how evenly a discussion or seminar was shared. Paste a transcript with speaker labels and see talk-time distribution, turn counts, questions asked, and an equity score that flags whether a few voices dominated.
Equity uses the Gini coefficient of words per speaker: 0 = perfectly even, 1 = one person did all the talking. Under ~0.4 is a well-shared discussion.
