Statistical Results Visualizer
Plot the kind of figures stats papers need: means with error bars (95% CI / SE / SD), or a forest plot of effect sizes with confidence intervals. Type your summary values and get a clean, labelled SVG you can drop straight into a manuscript. Local.
For the forest plot use Label | effect | lower | upper per line (e.g. odds ratio and its CI). A vertical null line marks no effect.
