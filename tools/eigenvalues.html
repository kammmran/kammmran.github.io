Eigenvalue & Eigenvector Calculator
Compute the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a real square matrix (up to 6×6) using the QR algorithm, plus the trace, determinant, and characteristic-polynomial checks. Enter the matrix and get real (and complex) eigenvalues with their vectors. Local.
Uses the unshifted QR iteration with Gram–Schmidt - robust for well-conditioned matrices; near-defective or highly non-normal matrices may converge slowly. Complex conjugate pairs are detected from 2×2 blocks on the quasi-triangular result.
