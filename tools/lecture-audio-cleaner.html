Lecture Audio Cleaner
Improve the clarity of a recorded lecture. Load an audio file, then apply a high-pass to cut rumble and hum, a low-pass to tame hiss, a noise gate to silence room tone between sentences, and a compressor + gain to even out the voice. Preview live and export a cleaned WAV — all in your browser.
Processing is real-time Web Audio — no upload. Export renders offline at full quality. The noise gate works on quiet gaps; for constant background noise, lean on the high-pass/low-pass filters.
