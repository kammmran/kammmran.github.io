CRISPR Guide Designer
Find candidate sgRNA guides in a target sequence. Scans both strands for the chosen PAM (SpCas9 NGG, SaCas9, Cas12a TTTV…), extracts 20-nt protospacers, scores them on GC content, poly-T terminators and homopolymer runs, and marks the predicted cut site. A first-pass design aid. Local.
On-target scoring here is a simple heuristic (GC window, no poly-T, no long runs). It does not predict off-targets - always run candidates through a genome-wide off-target tool (e.g. CRISPOR, Cas-OFFinder) before use.
