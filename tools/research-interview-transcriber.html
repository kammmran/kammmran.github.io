Research Interview Transcriber
Transcribe qualitative interviews live with speaker separation and timestamps. Tag who's speaking, then export as text or VTT. Runs on the browser's built-in speech recognition - audio never leaves your device.

Tip: switch the active speaker tag whenever a new person talks - speech recognition can't separate voices on its own, so you steer the labels. Double-click a tag to rename. Works best in Chrome/Edge in a quiet room.
