Figure Accessibility Checker
See your figure the way colour-blind readers do. Simulate protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia side by side, get a quick estimate of how reliant the figure is on red–green distinctions, and check it in grayscale to confirm it survives black-and-white printing. All processing is local.
The red–green reliance score estimates how much of the figure's colour information sits on the red/green axis that's hardest for the most common colour-blindness types (~8% of men). High reliance + survives-grayscale failing = add shapes, line styles, direct labels or a colour-blind-safe palette. Simulations use the Brettel/Viénot transform and are approximate.
