- Grew to 200+ active users, and students earned up to $500 collectively in its first six months
- Event tracking and opportunities shared through the website
- Went through the SABAH.HUB incubation (Jan - May 2024) and the UFAZ IGNITE incubation program (Oct 2023 - Jul 2024)

The platform was later sold to a company in our competition, expanding its reach and impact.
