A browser-based mouse colony simulation for exploring the links between behaviour, environment and modelled brain activity. Nine cages offer different conditions, with tunnels that let animals move between them.

Click inside the simulation to begin. Use **W/A/S/D** to move, **X** to watch another mouse and **K** to open the tunnels. Fullscreen gives you more room for the controls and brain panels.

- Interactive 3D arena and brain views built with Three.js
- Live neural activity, spatial maps and behavioural signals
- Environmental controls, virtual optogenetics and downloadable session reports

Designed for teaching and exploration; the simplified model is not intended for drawing scientific conclusions.

Source code: [mylovely-mice on GitHub](https://github.com/kammmran/mylovely-mice)
