M1 research internship (UFAZ / University of Strasbourg) in the Chemoinformatics and Computational Chemistry Research Group at ASOIU, supervised by Dr. Shamkhal Baybekov. Mar - Jun 2026.

## What I did

- **Data:** curated NIST ILThermo into three clean datasets - 5,136 density, 1,524 refractive index and 868 melting point compounds - with deduplication, pure-compound filtering, ambient-condition windows and RDKit standardisation.
- **Descriptors:** 1,613 Mordred 2D descriptors per compound, plus Bemis-Murcko scaffolds and an 88-pattern SMARTS catalogue of cation and anion classes.
- **Maps:** UMAP projections under three hyperparameter settings, shown in an interactive Plotly / SMILESDrawer dashboard.

## Findings

The density and refractive-index sets are over 92% near-duplicates, while the melting-point set is structurally distinct. For future QSPR work, scaffold- or cluster-based splits are a better test than random ones.
