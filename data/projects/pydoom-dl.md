A DOOM-style raycasting shooter written in Python, with a reinforcement-learning environment that lets an agent take over the controls. The game simulation is independent of the display, and textures, monsters and weapons are generated with NumPy.

- Playable levels with seven weapons and six monster types
- Gymnasium-compatible environment with vector and pixel observations
- PPO training with a live dashboard showing the agent's policy, rewards and progress
- Random and scripted agents for baseline comparisons

Source code and setup instructions: [PyDOOM-DL on GitHub](https://github.com/kammmran/PyDOOM-DL)
