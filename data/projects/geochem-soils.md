AI-driven modelling of how geochemical elements, heavy metals and PAHs move through soils in Azerbaijan's oil-producing regions, to support remediation planning. UFAZ Research Call 2026-2027.

## My role

- Building and optimising the machine learning models.
- Lab analysis of the organic and inorganic composition of soil samples.
- Processing and statistical analysis of the data.

## Approach

Random Forest and Gradient Boosting, combined with hybrid neural networks that use geochemical domain knowledge. Trained on samples from the Absheron Peninsula.

## Planned outputs

Validated predictive models, an open geochemical database and decision-support maps. Partner in France: Dr. Hugues Paucot (ICP-MS and elemental analysis).
